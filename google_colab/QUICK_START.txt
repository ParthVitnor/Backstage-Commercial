📋 THE SIMPLE 5-STEP PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━


STEP 1: Open Colab Notebook
────────────────────────────
1. Go to: https://colab.research.google.com/
2. Upload: BackstageCommercials_Colab.ipynb
3. Change runtime: GPU (T4)


STEP 2: Upload Your Files
──────────────────────────
Upload to Colab:
  • Your video (movie.mp4)
  • Product image (coffee.png)


STEP 3: Configure & Run
────────────────────────
1. Edit PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION = "Coffee jar"
2. Click "Runtime → Run all"
3. Wait ~15 minutes ☕


STEP 4: Download Result
────────────────────────
Colab downloads: final_video_with_product.mp4
Goes to: ~/Downloads/


STEP 5: Move to Frontend
─────────────────────────
Terminal:
  mv ~/Downloads/final_video_with_product.mp4 \
     frontend/prime-video-ui/public/videos/


DONE! ✅
────────
Start frontend: npm run dev
Open: http://localhost:5173
User watches video with ads!
