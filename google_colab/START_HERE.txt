🎬 WHAT THIS DOES

INPUT:
  • Your video (movie.mp4)
  • Product image (coffee.png)

PROCESSING (Automated on GPU):
  • Analyzes scenes
  • Finds best placement spot
  • Generates product with FLUX AI
  • Renders complete video

OUTPUT:
  • final_video_with_product.mp4
  • Product naturally embedded
  • Ready for users to watch!


📥 AFTER PROCESSING

1. Download video from Colab

2. Move to frontend folder:
   mv ~/Downloads/final_video_with_product.mp4 \
      frontend/prime-video-ui/public/videos/

3. Start frontend:
   cd frontend/prime-video-ui && npm run dev

4. Open: http://localhost:5173

5. Users watch video with ads! 🎉
